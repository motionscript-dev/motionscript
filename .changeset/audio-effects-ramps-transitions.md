Audio filters are now **audio effects**, automation is plain JSON, and a clip can fade in and out with a transition.

- **Renamed.** The `filters` prop of `SoundNode`, `AudioBus` and `timeline.sound` is `effects`, and `Video.audioFilters` is `audioEffects`. `AudioFilters` → `AudioEffects`, `AudioFilterChain` → `AudioEffectChain`, `AudioFilterItem` → `AudioEffect`, `AudioFilter` → `AudioEffectsInput`, `GainFilter` … `EchoFilter` → `GainEffect` … `EchoEffect` (and their `*Options`), `AudioFilterRegistry` → `AudioEffectRegistry`, `audioFiltersOps` → `audioEffectsOps`, `filtersKey` → `effectsKey`, `resolveAudioFilters` → `resolveAudioEffects`, `audioFiltersProperty` → `audioEffectsProperty` (descriptor kind `"audioEffects"`). `AudioRequest.filters` and `AudioBusStop.filters` are `effects`. In `@motionscript/browser`, `buildAudioFilterGraph` is `buildAudioEffectGraph`. A `sound` or `audioBus` row that still carries `filters` fails validation, naming the rename.
- **`speed` is a prop, not an effect.** The `speed` effect is gone; `SoundNode.speed` takes a number or a ramp (over seconds of the source), and `AudioRequest.speed` carries it. `effectiveSpeed`, `clipDurationFor` and `Sound.effectiveSpeed` are replaced by `clipTimeAtSource`, `sourceTimeAtClip` and `clipDurationOf`. A speed ramp is now placed on the clip's trimmed source (`AudioRequest.sourceLength`) on the device as on the timeline; the device used the whole decoded buffer, so a ramp on a clip trimmed short of the end played on a different axis from the one its length was computed on.
- **`Curve` is `Ramp`, and it is JSON**: `{ keys: [{ at, value, ease? } | { before, value, ease? }] }`, with `ease` an `EaseSpec`. A ramp survives a document round-trip, which a `Curve`'s ease functions did not. `ramp` and `hold` still build one fluently, and an engine-built ease is stored as its spec; an ease function the engine did not build throws. Segments before a bare `hold()` are placed from the start and segments after it from the end; a chain with no bare `hold()` now runs on from the start instead of pinning its last segment to the end. `fadeIn` and `fadeOut` are gone (use a transition); `isCurve` is `isRamp`, and `CurveSegment`/`ResolvedSegment` are `RampKey`/`ResolvedRampKey`. A tween on `speed` throws, as one on `volume` does.
- **Transitions.** `transitionIn` and `transitionOut` (`{ duration, curve? }`, on every audio node) fade a clip over its first or last seconds, on top of its `volume`. The curve defaults to equal power, so two overlapping clips, the first fading out and the second in over the overlap, cross-fade at a steady loudness. Requests and bus stops carry them, and the live device and the offline mix both apply them.
- A request's `volume` is a `Param`: a sound's ramped level is no longer folded into its effects as a gain.
- `EaseSpec` lives in `tween/ease/spec.ts`, with `easeOfSpec` (registry-free resolution) and `specOfEase`.
