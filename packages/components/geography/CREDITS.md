# Credits

The outlines in `data/` are derived from [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/)
v5.1.2 — `ne_10m_admin_0_countries_lakes`, `ne_10m_admin_0_countries_iso` and
`ne_10m_admin_1_states_provinces_lakes` — which is in the public domain.

`scripts/generate.mjs` merges, re-keys and simplifies them; `sources.lock.json`
pins the exact files it reads.
